03

How a job runs

The same sequence on a small powder room and on a full custom millwork build.

  1. 01

    Consultation and field verification

    We come out, measure, look at what is actually behind the finishes, and talk through what you want the room to be.

  2. 02

    A written scope

    You receive a written proposal listing the work item by item, what it costs, and what is excluded. You read it before anything is committed.

  3. 03

    Materials and selections

    Guidance on tile, fixtures, quantities and how finishes will work together. On larger work, a finish sample is reviewed before it goes on the job.

  4. 04

    The build

    Site protection, waterproofing, tile, plumbing and electrical within the scope, millwork, molding, paint and finish detailing. Staged payments tied to progress, set out in your proposal.

  5. 05

    Changes go in writing first

    If something is found behind a wall, or you want to add to the scope, it is priced and approved in writing before the work continues. No surprises on the final invoice.

  6. 06

    Walkthrough and warranty

    We walk the finished job with you, handle the punch list, and back the workmanship with a one-year warranty.

What a proposal will not include

  • On labor-only scopes, the finish materials and fixtures are yours to purchase — tile, vanity, faucet, toilet, mirror, light fixture, shower trim, molding and paint. We will tell you what to buy and how much of it.
  • Conditions nobody can see before demolition — water damage, deteriorated framing, subfloor damage, concealed plumbing or electrical problems. Found, reported, priced, then approved by you.
  • Work outside the room or scope you contracted, and specialty items quoted separately, such as decorative wall covering.
  • Architectural or engineering services, and permits, unless they are written into your proposal.

Most contractors leave this part out until the invoice. We put it in the proposal so you know exactly what you are buying.
