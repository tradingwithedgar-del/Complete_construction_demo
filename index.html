03

Every phase handled

Most renovations change hands three or four times, and the gaps between trades are where jobs go wrong. Pick a phase to see what we do in it.

Consultation & scope

We come out, measure, look at what is actually behind the finishes, and talk through what you want the house to become. You leave that conversation with a written scope.

  • Field verification of existing conditions
  • Scope written item by item
  • Guidance on materials and quantities
  • Staged payments set against progress

Demo & prep

Protecting the rest of the house is part of the job. Floors, doorways and the route in and out get covered before anything comes apart.

  • Protection of work areas and access routes
  • Removal of existing finishes and fixtures
  • Debris handling coordinated up front
  • Existing conditions inspected once opened up

Framing & substrate

What sits behind the tile decides whether the tile lasts. Framing, blocking and substrate are set true before a single sheet goes up.

  • Structural carpentry and framing
  • Blocking for rails, niches and vanities
  • Shower substrate installation
  • Subfloor preparation and levelling

Waterproofing

The phase nobody sees and the one that costs the most to get wrong. Wet areas are waterproofed properly before tile, every time.

  • Professional waterproofing of wet areas
  • Corners, curbs and penetrations detailed
  • Niches and benches sealed before tiling
  • Plumbing adjustments within the contracted scope

Tile & stone

Layout is decided before the first tile is set, so patterns land where they should and cuts fall where nobody looks.

  • Ceramic, porcelain, marble and mosaic
  • Large-format and stacked wall tile
  • Herringbone, hex and patterned floors
  • Grouting and finish detailing

Fixtures & power

Vanities, tapware, mirrors and lighting set, connected and tested before we call a room done.

  • Vanity, faucet, toilet and mirror installation
  • Shower body and trim connection
  • Light fixture installation and connection
  • Kitchen counters and kitchen lighting
  • Final fixture adjustment and testing

Millwork & finish

The same shop that builds architectural millwork to measure also runs the molding, paint and caulk on a powder room.

  • Custom millwork, designed and fabricated
  • Cornice, crown and panelled detailing
  • Built-ins, trim and wainscot
  • Professional staining and protective finishing
  • Decorative molding, paint and finish caulking

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished work with you and handle the punch list before the last payment, not after it.

  • Room-by-room walkthrough with you
  • Punch list agreed and completed
  • Jobsite cleanup and debris removal
  • Care notes for new finishes
04

What your proposal says

Before any work is booked you get a written scope with both columns filled in. The right-hand one is the reason the final invoice matches the first number.

Included

A
  • Every item of work, listed line by line
  • Protection of the work area and access routes
  • Guidance on what to buy, how much, and what works together
  • Payments staged against progress, not taken up front
  • A final walkthrough and punch list with you

Not included

B
  • On labor-only scopes, the finish materials are yours to buy: tile, vanity, faucet, toilet, mirror, light fixture, shower trim, molding and paint
  • Anything hidden until demolition: water damage, deteriorated framing, subfloor damage, concealed plumbing or electrical faults
  • Work outside the room or scope you contracted
  • Specialty items quoted separately, such as decorative wall covering
  • Architectural or engineering services, and permits, unless written in

Anything found behind a wall is priced and approved by you in writing before we carry on. That is the whole mechanism, and it is why nobody gets a surprise at the end.
